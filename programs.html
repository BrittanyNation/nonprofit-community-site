Impact, program by program

Pantry

Choice, not handouts

Neighbors shop the shelves themselves. Last year (sample figures): 18,400 visits, 62% produce and fresh food, and a “request shelf” that stocks what families actually ask for.

Meals

Dinner that builds a block

620 meals a month at shared tables, cooked with pantry surplus — so good food is eaten together instead of wasted, and regulars become the welcome crew.

Classes & Bags

Skills that stay home

38 classes taught and 1,150 weekend bags packed last year — small, repeatable help that carries a family from one week to the next.

How impact is reported here: every number on this page is a sample figure for this fictional concept — and the design rule behind it is real: label the time period, say what was counted, and never publish a number the program cannot explain in one sentence.
