Design notes

Help first, story second

Hours, eligibility in plain words (“no paperwork”), and the donate/volunteer path sit above the mission story — a nonprofit site serves people in a hurry before it persuades anyone.

Trust through specifics

Impact is shown as four labeled numbers and one human story, not a wall of badges. On the Get Involved page, exactly where a gift goes is stated before the amount buttons.

Warmth without softness

Deep forest carries authority, ochre carries the call to action, and warm sand keeps long pages gentle — badges and selected states always pair color with words, never color alone.
