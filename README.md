# Harbor & Hope Community Kitchen — Nonprofit Site UI Concept

An original nonprofit website UI concept for a fictional community kitchen and food pantry: a home page built around finding help fast, a programs & impact page with hours and plain-word eligibility, and a get-involved page where gift amounts are tied to real outcomes and volunteer roles state their hours up front.

**Live demo:** https://brittanynation.github.io/nonprofit-community-site/

## Roles this supports

Built to support applications for **UI Designer**, **Front-End Designer**, **Web Designer**, and **Junior UI/UX Designer** roles — nonprofit and mission-driven website work that sits between visual design and front-end implementation.

## What it demonstrates

- **Nonprofit information architecture** — one job per page: Home ("find help or give help fast"), Programs & Impact ("what happens, when, and for whom"), Get Involved ("give money, time, or groceries")
- **Help-first hierarchy** — pantry hours, "no paperwork" eligibility in plain words, and the donate/volunteer path sit above the mission story, because a nonprofit site serves people in a hurry first
- **Trust through specifics** — four labeled impact numbers, impact reported program by program with a stated reporting rule, and gift amounts that name what they cover before the donate button
- **Designed states, not just screens** — a pre-selected gift amount with badge, border, and label, plus volunteer roles that state shift length and the first-step process before any form appears
- **Honest form design** — mock donate and volunteer forms that say what an email will be used for before submission, mark optional fields, and state plainly that no real donation or submission happens
- **Accessibility-minded choices** — contrast-safe pairings, badges and selected states that use words and borders, never color alone, skip link, `aria-current` navigation, a real `<table>` for the weekly schedule, and real labels on every field
- **Responsive layout** — stat and card grids step down cleanly on small screens, and the schedule table remains readable on phones
- **Semantic, framework-free build** — hand-written HTML/CSS, one stylesheet, no JavaScript required, no external images; all visuals are CSS-only
