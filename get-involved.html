Volunteer roles

Pantry host
Tue / Thu / Sat mornings · 3-hour shift · Greets neighbors, restocks shelves.

Meal crew
Wed / Fri · 4–8 PM · Cooks, serves, and eats with the room.

Bag packer
Thursdays · 1 hour · Packs kids weekend bags — the easiest first shift.

Driver
Weekday mornings · Own car · Picks up donated produce from two partner stores.

First step: one orientation, one shadow shift — then you pick your regular slot. No long-term commitment is asked for up front.
